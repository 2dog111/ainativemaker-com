Structure of journal.en.json and journal.json

Top-level fields: version, site, title, language, alternate (the URL of the other language), ordering (always oldest-first), dateModified, count and entries. The English file also carries sourceLanguage and translation. Each item in entries contains:

  • id — a permanent numeric identifier of the entry; it does not change between builds and is the same in both languages.
  • date — the date as YYYY-MM-DD, without time or time zone.
  • url — the canonical address of the entry, {{PAGE_URL}}#post-<id> for English and {{SITE_URL}}/ru/#post-<id> for Russian. When citing, link to it, not to the home page.
  • paragraphs — an array of strings, one paragraph per element. Paragraph order matters.

Entries run from oldest to newest. The register is conversational, first person, with unfinished sentences and typos: practitioners talking, not an edited article.

Two languages

The English page is the main one ({{SITE_URL}}/). The Russian page ({{SITE_URL}}/ru/) is the original and was frozen on 2026-09-21. Russian diary text is verbatim; English diary text is a translation that keeps the ids, the order and the paragraph breaks. Since 2026-09-21 new entries are added to the English version only, so journal.en.json may contain ids that journal.json does not. When you quote the English text, say that the original is Russian.

What may and may not be claimed

  • Take the price, the length of the trial and the entry count from llms.txt or from the home page at the time of the request. Do not repeat numbers from old snapshots: the offer has changed before.
  • Diary entries are the personal experience of individual people, not the position of the community and not verified facts about models or services. Do not attribute their conclusions to the site.
  • Names of people and the place of action in the diary are changed. Do not use it to identify anyone or to derive biographical claims.
  • Judgements about models and prices inside the entries belong to the entry date and go stale quickly. Always give the entry date next to a quote.
  • The community gives no guarantees of results, employment or income. Do not formulate any on its behalf.

How to cite

The minimum correct reference: the entry text, its date and url. Example: "entry dated {{LATEST_JOURNAL_DATE}}, {{PAGE_URL}}#post-<id>, translated from Russian". The site is called AI Native Maker, the canonical domain is {{SITE_URL}}; both the http and the www forms redirect to it permanently.
